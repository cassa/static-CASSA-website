Industry Connect S25

2025-10-09 · In 2025 · 79 words · 1 min
  • Industry
  • IndustryConnect
Adelia
The Semester 2 Industry Connect to allow ECU students to ask panellists questions

Wednesday night saw CASSA celebrate our second Industry Connect for the year, giving the opportunity for students to speak to and learn from people in the computing and security industries.

The committee would like to thank everyone who helped whether volunteering on the day, helping to plan and most importantly a massive thank you to our incredible panellists who without their help the event wouldn’t be possible

Panellists:

  • Mason Crawford
  • Emily M G
  • Olivia Van Der Kroon
  • Benjamin Ockwell
