## Who can be a member?
Any ECU student can become a member of CASSA, you don't even need to be studying a computing/security course to become a member, just be interested in tech/security and have an open mind.

## How to become a member?
Becomming a member only takes two simple steps:
1. Fill out the form embeded below
2. Join [our discord <i class="fa-brands fa-discord"></i>](https://discord.gg/6bmQ9NerwT)
